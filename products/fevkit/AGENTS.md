# FEVKit project rules

- Preserve Function, Evidence, and Validation as separate axes.
- Never convert the profile into a single trust score.
- A reproducible run is not automatically scientifically correct.
- Do not fabricate missing provenance, evidence, uncertainty, review, or validation.
- Keep audit local and replay opt-in.
- Retain negative results and failed gates.
- Do not claim clinical validity, regulatory compliance, standards certification, external adoption, or endorsement without direct evidence.
