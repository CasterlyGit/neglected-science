# Maintenance policy

- The run spec is versioned independently from the package.
- Deterministic rules require adversarial fixtures.
- Profile changes require a documented rationale and backward-compatibility note.
- Replay stays opt-in and never becomes an implicit audit side effect.
- Browser behavior remains local-only unless a separate hosted product is explicitly designed and reviewed.
- Standards identifiers and conformance language are reviewed whenever upstream profiles change.
- External claims are recorded only after direct evidence is archived.
