# Why now

Scientific agents are moving from isolated question answering toward multi-tool execution. The abstraction boundary for their run records is still forming. If every framework keeps a proprietary or idiosyncratic trace, review and interoperability become harder after adoption is entrenched.

FEVKit does not attempt to own the agent, tools, datasets, or scientific decision. It focuses on the narrow layer that can remain portable: what ran, what evidence was used, what claims were made, what was checked, and what validation stage was actually demonstrated.

This timing thesis remains a hypothesis until upstream teams use or reject the adapter contract.
