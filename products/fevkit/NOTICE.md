# Notice and attribution

FEVKit is an independent open-source implementation of trajectory-assurance ideas for scientific and biomedical agents. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the authors or maintainers of any referenced paper, agent framework, benchmark, RO-Crate profile, or biomedical platform.

Function, Evidence, and Validation terminology is used descriptively and should be reviewed with the relevant research authors before any claim of formal framework conformance. RO-Crate and Process Run profile identifiers are used for interoperability; the current exporter is not a certification claim.
