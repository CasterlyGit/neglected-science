# System-2-ready corpus contract

A System-1 record may enter System 2 only when it contains all of the following: a primary-source claim and location; official data and acquisition state; system, unit, measurement, and published baseline; published inference and claim boundary; named fragility and alternative explanation; independent check; and a material mismatch retained in the record.

Every source-audit record produces exactly one output: a System-2-ready structural record, a failed-gate rejection receipt, or an interview-ready domain-specific candidate packet. Source count, survival rate, and apparent cross-domain similarity are not quality measures.

Before candidate admission, the deterministic System-2 shadow review maps the listed roles, preserves mismatches, requires a domain-specific consequence, and emits one plain-language non-promoting verdict plus one permitted next action. `LIMITED SUPPORT — NO TARGET TRIO` is a valid outcome when it is evidence-traceable. System 1 is therefore rewarded for increasing the future verdict's discriminating power, not for steering it toward support or promotion.
