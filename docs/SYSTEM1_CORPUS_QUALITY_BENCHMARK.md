# System-1 corpus-quality benchmark

System 1 is evaluated on a predeclared, independently audited corpus-quality contract—not candidate survival, positive results, or a desired cross-domain conclusion.

Each evaluated record must have all of: a traceable primary claim and location; official data and acquisition evidence; mapped system, unit, measurement, baseline, inference, and validation gap; named fragility and alternative explanation; independent check or explicit infeasibility; material mismatch; and a correct gate disposition. Rejection receipts count as successful records when their failed gate is correct and traceable.

System 1 also improves the *quality of the future System-2 verdict*. A passing record must make the eventual verdict more discriminating: it must say what relationship is supported, what larger claim remains prohibited, and what one next action is permitted. This rewards clearer falsification, source traceability, and decision value whether the later verdict is supportive, null, contrary, or a stop; it never rewards a preferred scientific conclusion.

The benchmark uses ten held-out, blinded records selected before the relevant process version is run. An independent audit scores each criterion without seeing the system disposition first. The target is an 80–90% all-criteria pass rate. Below 80% triggers one named bottleneck and one versioned process improvement; above 90% triggers a leniency audit rather than a success claim. System 2 receives only records that pass this benchmark contract and its necessary shadow handoff check.
