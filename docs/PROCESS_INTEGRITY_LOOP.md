# Process-integrity loop

## Reward target

The system is rewarded only for a **verified final method verdict**: either a reproducible investigation with bounded method learning, or an evidence-backed redesign-or-stop decision. Candidate volume, apparent novelty, positive results, corpus size, and speed are not rewards.

## Noncompensatory controls

- A source record, metadata record, score, or cross-domain analogy cannot become a target.
- Selection requires exactly three interview-ready targets and two first-interview survivors: a primary and reserve.
- Fresh official acquisition, integrity, and claim boundaries remain gates; a prior download, cache, or missing review does not compensate.
- Rejections, access failures, nulls, and uncertainty are retained and count as information.
- The evaluator may recommend only `continue-bounded-cycle`, `ready-for-selection`, or `redesign-or-stop`; it cannot promote a scientific claim.

## Operating behavior

Run small, deliberately diverse source-feasibility batches. Pause after each trio-level gate, evaluate the failure pattern, and change one approved process control only when the record shows a repeatable failure mode. A run is complete only when the method verdict, its sources, its gates, and its limitations are reproducible locally.
