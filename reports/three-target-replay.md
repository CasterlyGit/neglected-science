## What changed

The pilot treated mechanically generated questions as candidates and deferred actual prior-art, protocol, and consequence checks until Milestone 6. The revised factory treats those outputs only as raw leads. A lead becomes one of the three targets only after all five checks pass:

1. consequential distance from the closest primary experiment;
2. verified variables and protocol from official documentation and a real sample;
3. scientific value under positive, null, and contrary outcomes;
4. a test that distinguishes the claim from the strongest simple explanation; and
5. bounded specialist dependencies.

Any failure is noncompensatory. The factory must replace the lead; ranking cannot average the defect away.

## Interpretation

This replay does not prove the next generated trio will survive. It proves the known pilot failures are now prevented at candidate creation rather than rediscovered after an expensive funnel. Real first-interview and confirmation rejections remain scientifically valid and must not be suppressed.

The next authorized action is not to reuse or rewrite these three. It is to forge replacement raw leads until exactly three genuinely interview-ready, diverse targets pass the construction contract, then interview that trio.
