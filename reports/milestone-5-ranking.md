## Finalists

### 1. Urban thermal recovery from ECOSTRESS and Landsat context

Question: Can irregular-time ECOSTRESS observations, anchored by Landsat baselines, estimate neighborhood thermal recovery curves after heat events, and do canopy and impervious form explain between-neighborhood differences?

Primary gate for Milestone 6: verify overpass/event feasibility, distinguish surface from air-temperature claims, and search prior work using thermal persistence, nocturnal heat, cooling rate, and diurnal-cycle terminology.

### 2. Battery relaxation signatures and path-dependent degradation

Question: Do rest-period relaxation signatures in NASA battery cycling data improve prediction of path-dependent degradation beyond models based only on cumulative exposure summaries?

Primary gate for Milestone 6: audit cell protocols and rest periods, search prior work on order effects and relaxation features, and determine whether comparable interventions exist.

### 3. Cross-domain recovery-curve transferability

Question: After aligning perturbation and state definitions, are recovery-curve features—rate, hysteresis, and incomplete return—mathematically transferable across urban heat, ecosystems, batteries, and cyclic materials, or do domain-specific mechanisms dominate?

Primary gate for Milestone 6: aggressively search domain-specific equivalents and be prepared to narrow to a pairwise comparison or reject the translation entirely.
